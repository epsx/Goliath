# Metadata license routing

The bundled catalogs live under `../../metadata/` and retain their embedded
file-specific notices.

- The Geolith-derived `geolith.xml` embeds the complete Copyright 2024 orbea
  redistribution notice and disclaimer.
- The MAME `neogeo.xml` and `neocd.xml` headers declare CC0-1.0. The complete
  CC0 text is retained at `../upstream/jgrf/CC0-1.0.txt`.
- MAME `softwarelist.dtd` is treated conservatively as GPL-2.0-or-later. The
  complete GPL text is retained at
  `../msys2/qt6-base/GPL-2.0-or-later.txt`.

Exact upstream references and snapshot SHA-256 values are recorded in
`../../metadata/README.md`.
